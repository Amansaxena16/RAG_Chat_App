# NovaTech Leave and Time-Off Policy

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

All full-time NovaTech employees accrue Paid Time Off (PTO) at a rate of 1.75 days per month, totaling 21 days per year. PTO accrual begins on an employee's start date and unused PTO carries over into the next calendar year up to a maximum cap of 10 days. Any PTO balance beyond the 10-day cap is forfeited on December 31st unless local law requires otherwise.

Employees must request PTO at least 5 business days in advance through the HR portal (people.novatech.io) and receive manager approval before the time off is confirmed. Emergency leave requests are handled case-by-case by an employee's direct manager.

## Public Holidays

NovaTech observes public holidays according to the employee's home office location:

- **Bengaluru office**: 10 public holidays per year, following the Karnataka state holiday calendar.
- **Austin office**: 8 public holidays per year, following the standard US federal holiday calendar plus Juneteenth.
- **Lisbon office**: 13 public holidays per year, following the Portuguese national holiday calendar.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year, which do not carry over and do not require advance notice. For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, a doctor's note may be requested by HR.

## Parental Leave

NovaTech offers 18 weeks of fully paid parental leave for the primary caregiver and 8 weeks of fully paid leave for the secondary caregiver, regardless of gender, for births, adoptions, or fostering. Parental leave can be taken continuously or split into two blocks within the first 12 months after the qualifying event, subject to manager approval on timing.

## Sabbatical Leave

Employees who have completed 5 continuous years at NovaTech are eligible for a 4-week paid sabbatical. Sabbaticals must be scheduled at least 3 months in advance and coordinated with the employee's manager to ensure coverage.

## Unpaid Leave of Absence

In cases where an employee has exhausted PTO and sick leave, NovaTech may grant an unpaid leave of absence for up to 90 days, subject to VP-level approval and HR review. Benefits continuation during unpaid leave varies by country and is handled by the People Operations team.
