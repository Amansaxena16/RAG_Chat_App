# NovaTech Pricing and Plans

NovaTech pricing is per-seat, billed monthly or annually (annual billing gets a 15% discount). Pricing below is per user, per month, billed annually.

## Starter Plan — $8/user/month

- Access to one product (NovaBoard, NovaChat, or NovaSync — customer's choice).
- Up to 25 users.
- 5 GB storage per user (NovaSync only).
- Community support (help center + community forum).
- No automation rules, no unified search.

## Growth Plan — $16/user/month

- Access to all three products (NovaBoard, NovaChat, NovaSync).
- Unlimited users.
- 50 GB storage per user (NovaSync).
- Unified search across all three products.
- Automation rules (up to 20 active rules per Space in NovaBoard).
- Email support with a 24-hour response SLA.
- 90-day version history in NovaSync.

## Enterprise Plan — Custom pricing

- Everything in Growth, plus:
- Single sign-on (SSO) via SAML and SCIM user provisioning.
- Unlimited automation rules.
- Unlimited version history in NovaSync.
- Dedicated customer success manager.
- 4-hour response SLA with a named support contact.
- Custom data residency options (currently: US, EU, or India).
- Audit log export and advanced admin controls.

## Add-ons (Growth and Enterprise only)

- **Extra storage**: $2/user/month per additional 50 GB in NovaSync.
- **Advanced analytics**: $4/user/month, adds cross-product reporting dashboards (e.g., cycle time in NovaBoard correlated with message volume in NovaChat).

## Nonprofit and Education Discount

Verified nonprofit organizations and educational institutions receive 30% off the Growth plan. This discount does not apply to Enterprise plan custom pricing, which is negotiated separately.

## Trial and Cancellation

All plans include a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan features, no credit card required to start. Customers can cancel at any time; annual plans are refunded on a prorated basis only within the first 30 days of an annual commitment.

## Note on Terminology

"Plan" in this document always refers to a NovaTech subscription tier (Starter, Growth, Enterprise). This is unrelated to "leave plan" or "ramp plan," which are HR terms describing employee time off and onboarding schedules — see the HR documents for those.
