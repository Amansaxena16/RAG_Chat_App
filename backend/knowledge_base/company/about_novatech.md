# About NovaTech Systems

## Company Overview

NovaTech Systems Inc. is a business software company founded in 2018 by Priya Ramanathan and Diego Alvarez in Bengaluru, India. The company builds collaboration and productivity tools for small and mid-sized businesses. NovaTech now has offices in Bengaluru, Austin, and Lisbon, and employs approximately 340 people across engineering, sales, customer success, and operations.

NovaTech's mission is "to make coordinated work feel effortless." The company was started after its founders experienced firsthand, while working at a logistics startup, how much time teams lose switching between chat, task boards, and documents that don't talk to each other.

## Products

NovaTech's flagship products are:

- **NovaBoard** — a Kanban-style project management tool with automation rules.
- **NovaChat** — a team messaging app with built-in video huddles.
- **NovaSync** — a file synchronization and document collaboration service.

These three products share a single sign-on system and a unified search index, which is one of NovaTech's key differentiators against competitors who sell similar tools as unrelated products.

## Funding and Growth

NovaTech raised a Seed round in 2018, a Series A in 2020 led by Meridian Ventures, and a Series B in 2023 led by Blackwood Capital, totaling $62 million raised. As of the most recent fiscal year, NovaTech reports over 14,000 paying customer organizations and annual recurring revenue (ARR) in the range of $40-45 million.

## Company Values

NovaTech's stated company values are:

1. **Default to transparency** — internal documents, roadmaps, and even most Slack channels are open to all employees unless there is a specific reason (legal, HR, security) to restrict them.
2. **Ship small, ship often** — engineering teams are encouraged to deploy multiple times per day rather than batching changes into large releases.
3. **Customers are not tickets** — support and success teams are evaluated on customer outcomes, not ticket-closure speed.
4. **Write it down** — decisions of consequence are expected to be documented, not just discussed verbally or in meetings.
