# NovaTech Leadership Team

## Executive Team

**Priya Ramanathan — Co-Founder & CEO**
Priya leads company strategy, fundraising, and the executive team. Before NovaTech, she was a product manager at a logistics startup called RouteWise, where she met co-founder Diego Alvarez. She holds a B.Tech in Computer Science from IIT Madras. Priya is based in the Bengaluru office.

**Diego Alvarez — Co-Founder & CTO**
Diego owns NovaTech's overall technical architecture and engineering culture. He previously worked as a backend engineer at RouteWise and, before that, at a payments company in São Paulo. Diego is based in the Austin office and splits his time between Austin and Bengaluru.

**Fatima Khalil — Chief Financial Officer**
Fatima joined NovaTech in 2021 from a Series C fintech company, where she was VP of Finance. She oversees financial planning, the Series B fundraising process, and investor relations. Fatima is based in Lisbon.

**Marcus Chen — VP of Engineering**
Marcus manages the three engineering pods (NovaBoard, NovaChat, NovaSync) and reports to Diego Alvarez. He joined NovaTech in 2019 as a senior backend engineer and was promoted to VP in 2022. Marcus is based in Austin.

**Ingrid Solberg — VP of Customer Success**
Ingrid leads onboarding, support, and renewals across all regions. She previously ran customer success at a Norwegian SaaS company. Ingrid is based in Lisbon.

**Rahul Deshpande — VP of Sales**
Rahul leads the global sales organization, including enterprise and self-serve motions. He is based in Bengaluru and travels frequently to Austin.

## Reporting Structure Notes

Engineering managers for each product pod report to Marcus Chen, not directly to Diego Alvarez. Diego is only looped in for architecture decisions that cross pod boundaries (for example, the unified search index shared by NovaBoard, NovaChat, and NovaSync).

The Customer Success and Sales organizations operate independently but hold a joint weekly sync to review at-risk accounts, led jointly by Ingrid Solberg and Rahul Deshpande.
